How this list was made

Each link dated 2026-10-21 was fetched on that day, or confirmed through a browser when a site blocks scripts, and kept only if it loaded and matched its description. The date beside a link says when it was last checked; a link marked “Not yet checked” has not been opened. Some sources appear under more than one chapter. The notes are written for this book; the material itself belongs to its authors.

Links rot. If one is dead, search for its title; if you replace it, update the checked date. Levels: beginner needs only earlier chapters, intermediate assumes the chapter it sits under, advanced is research-level or long.
