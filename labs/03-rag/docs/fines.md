# Charges for late, lost and damaged items

## Late returns

Items on an adult card that come back after their due date are charged 25p per item for each day they are overdue, up to a maximum of £5 per item. Items on children's and teenage cards are never charged for being late.

## When your card is blocked

If you owe £10 or more, you cannot borrow, renew or reserve items until you have paid enough to bring the amount below £10. You can still use the computers, the Wi-Fi and the e-library.

## Lost or damaged items

If you lose an item or it comes back damaged, you pay its replacement cost, shown in My account, plus a £2 processing fee. If you find a lost item and return it within three months of paying, we refund the replacement cost but not the processing fee.

## How to pay

Pay online in My account with a debit or credit card, at the self-service kiosks in every branch, or at any desk by card or cash.

## Having a charge removed

We remove late charges if you could not return items because you were in hospital, were bereaved, or had another serious difficulty. Ask at any desk or use the contact form; you do not need to send proof.
