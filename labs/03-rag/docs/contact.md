# Contact us and feedback

## Phone and email

Phone 01632 960418 from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday, or email enquiries@brindlemere-library.example at any time. We answer emails within two working days.

## Complaints

If something has gone wrong, tell us through the contact form on the website or in a letter to Central Library. We reply within 10 working days. If you are not happy with our reply, you can ask for your complaint to be reviewed by the Head of Libraries.

## Lost property

Things left in a library are kept at that branch for four weeks. After that, valuables are handed to the police and everything else is given to charity.
