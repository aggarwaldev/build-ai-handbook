# E-books, e-audiobooks and e-magazines

## Getting started

Download the Brindlemere Reads app on your phone or tablet, or use the e-library website on a computer. Sign in with your library card number and your PIN. Visitor cards cannot use the e-library.

## Loans and limits

You can borrow up to 8 e-books and e-audiobooks at a time. Each loan lasts 14 days and ends by itself, so there are never late charges. If you finish early, return the title in the app so the next person can have it.

## E-magazines

E-magazines do not count towards your limit of 8. You can read as many as you like, and issues you download stay in the app for as long as you want them.

## Devices

The app works on most phones and tablets. Many e-readers work too, but some need you to download the book on a computer first and copy it across with a cable.

## Forgotten PIN

Reset your PIN on the e-library website with the email address on your library account, or ask at any desk. Staff cannot tell you your old PIN, only set a new one.
