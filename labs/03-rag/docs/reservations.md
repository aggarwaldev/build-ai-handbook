# Reserving items

## Placing a reservation

Reserve any item in our catalogue online, in the library app or at a desk, and choose the branch where you want to collect it. A reservation costs 50p on an adult card and is free on a child's card. You can have up to 20 reservations at a time.

## Collecting a reservation

We email or text you when your item is ready. It waits for you at the collection branch for seven days. If you do not collect it in that time, it goes to the next person or back to the shelf, and the 50p is not refunded.

## Books we do not own

If we do not have a book, we can borrow it from another library service for you. This interlibrary loan costs £4 per book and usually takes two to four weeks. The lending library sets the loan period, and these loans cannot always be renewed.
