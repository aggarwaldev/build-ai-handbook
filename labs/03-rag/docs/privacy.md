# Your data and privacy

## What we keep about you

We keep your name, address, date of birth, contact details and the items you have on loan or reserved now. We use them to run your library account and nothing else, and we never sell them.

## Your borrowing history

When you return an item, we delete the link between you and that item, so we do not keep a list of what you have read. If you would like a list, switch on Reading history in My account and we will keep one until you switch it off.

## How long we keep your account

If you do not use your card for three years, we close your account and delete your details, unless you still owe us money or have items on loan.

## Seeing your data

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you. It is free, and we send it within one month.
