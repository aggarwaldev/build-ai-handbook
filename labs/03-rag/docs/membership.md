# Joining the library

## Who can join

Anyone who lives, works or studies in Brindlemere can join the library. Membership is free. People who are only visiting the city can have a visitor card instead.

## What to bring

To join at a branch, bring photo ID and proof of your address dated within the last three months, such as a utility bill or a tenancy agreement. You can also join online: you get a card number straight away and can use the e-library at once. Collect your plastic card from any branch within 30 days, bringing the same documents.

## Children's cards

Children under 16 can join for free with the signature of a parent or carer. A child's card borrows from the children's and teenage collections. Young people aged 12 to 15 can also borrow from the adult collection if a parent or carer agrees on the form. Children's cards never pay late charges.

## Visitor cards

A visitor card is for people staying in Brindlemere for less than three months. It is free, lasts three months and borrows up to three items at a time. It cannot be used for the e-library or to reserve items.

## Lost cards

Tell us as soon as you lose your card, by phone or at any branch, and we will block it so nobody else can use it. Your first replacement card is free. After that, each replacement costs £2.50.

## Changing your details

Update your address, email or phone number in My account on the library website, or ask at any desk. Please keep your email address current: it is how we send reminders and tell you when a reservation is ready.
