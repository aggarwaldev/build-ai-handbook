# Opening hours

## Central Library

Central Library is open from 9am to 8pm Monday to Friday, from 9am to 5pm on Saturday, and from 11am to 4pm on Sunday.

## Northgate Library

Northgate Library is open from 10am to 6pm on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and from 10am to 1pm on Saturday. It is closed on Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday.

## Riverside Library

Riverside Library is open from 9.30am to 5pm Tuesday to Saturday. It is closed on Sunday and Monday.

## Bank holidays and Christmas

All branches are closed on bank holidays. Over Christmas, branches close at 1pm on 24 December and 31 December, and are closed on 25 and 26 December and on 1 January. The e-library stays open all year.
