# Events and activities

## For young children

Rhyme Time, for babies and toddlers under 2 with their grown-ups, is at 10.30am every Tuesday at Central Library. Story Time, for children aged 3 to 5, is at 11am every Saturday in all three branches. Both are free and you do not need to book.

## Summer Reading Challenge

Every summer holiday, children aged 4 to 11 are challenged to read six library books. They collect stickers as they go, and everyone who finishes gets a medal and a certificate. Sign up at any branch from the start of July.

## Code Club

Code Club is for children aged 9 to 13. It meets at Riverside Library from 4.30pm to 5.30pm every Thursday in school term time. Places are free but must be booked, and we provide the laptops.

## For adults

The reading group meets at Central Library on the first Monday of each month at 7pm. Knit and Natter is on Friday afternoons at Northgate. The English Conversation Cafe, for people learning English, meets at Northgate at 2pm every Wednesday.
