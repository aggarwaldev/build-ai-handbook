# Booking a room

## Rooms you can book

Central Library has the Garden Room, which seats 30 people, and four study pods for up to 4 people each. Riverside Library has the Community Hall, which seats 60.

## Prices

Study pods are free for library members, for up to two hours a day. The Garden Room costs £15 an hour for charities and community groups and £30 an hour for businesses. The Community Hall costs £20 an hour for charities and community groups and £40 an hour for businesses.

## How to book

Book the Garden Room and the Community Hall online at least two working days ahead. Study pods can be booked online or at the Central desk on the same day.

## Cancelling a booking

Cancel at least 48 hours before the start of your booking and we refund the full amount. If you cancel later than that, or do not turn up, we cannot give a refund.

## Equipment and food

The Garden Room has a projector, a screen and a hearing loop. The Community Hall has a kitchen with a kettle and fridge. You may bring cold food and drinks into either room; hot food is not allowed.
