# Donating books

## What we accept

We welcome books in good condition that were published in the last five years, and children's picture books of any age. Please bring no more than one bag at a time, to any branch desk.

## What we cannot take

We cannot take encyclopaedias, textbooks, magazines, videos, or books that are damaged, damp or written in. We have nowhere to store them, and we would have to pay to dispose of them.

## What happens to donations

Staff choose some donated books to add to the library's stock. The rest are sold at the book sale in the Central Library entrance, and the money goes to the Friends of Brindlemere Library, who use it to pay for children's events.
