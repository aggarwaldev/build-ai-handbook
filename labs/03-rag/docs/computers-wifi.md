# Computers, Wi-Fi and printing

## Public computers

Library members can use our computers for up to two hours a day for free. Book a computer online or at a desk. People without a library card can buy a guest pass at the desk for £1 an hour.

## Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is free in every branch, with no time limit. Join the network called Brindlemere-Library; there is no password, but you need to accept the terms on the first page that opens.

## Printing and copying

Printing and photocopying cost 15p a page in black and white and 50p a page in colour, for A4 or A3. You can print from our computers, or from your own laptop or phone through the print portal on the library website, and collect your pages at the desk.

## Scanning

Scanning is free. Every branch has a scanner that sends the scan to your email address or saves it to a USB stick.
