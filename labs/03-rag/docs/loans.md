# Borrowing

## How many items

An adult card can have up to 15 items on loan at once, of which no more than 4 can be DVDs. A child's card can have up to 10 items. A visitor card can have up to 3.

## Loan periods

You can keep books and audiobooks on CD for three weeks, and DVDs for one week. Express books, the new bestsellers on the shelf by the entrance, are lent for one week and cannot be renewed, so that more people get to read them.

## Renewing

You can renew an item up to three times, online in My account, in the library app or by phone. Each renewal gives you a full new loan period from the day you renew. You cannot renew an item that another member has reserved; it must come back by its due date.

## Returning items

Return items to any of our three branches, not only the one you borrowed from. The book drop outside Central Library is open 24 hours a day. Items left in the book drop after closing time are checked in the next morning, and we do not charge for the night in between.

## Due-date reminders

We email you three days before an item is due, and again on the day it is due. You can switch the reminders off in My account, but late charges still apply if you do.
