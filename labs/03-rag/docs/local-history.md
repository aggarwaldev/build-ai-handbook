# Local history and archives

## Visiting the Local Studies room

The Local Studies room is on the second floor of Central Library and is open from 10am to 4pm Tuesday to Saturday. You can use the open shelves without an appointment. To see original documents, book an appointment at least three days ahead.

## What the collection holds

We hold maps of Brindlemere from the 1820s onwards, local newspapers on microfilm from 1850, copies of parish registers, electoral registers, and more than 20,000 photographs of the city and its people.

## Photographs and copies

You may photograph most items with your own camera or phone for free, as long as you do not use a flash. Staff can make high-quality scans for £1.50 per image. Some items cannot be copied because of their condition or copyright.

## Research by email

If you cannot visit, email us your question. Staff will spend up to 30 minutes on it for free. Longer research is charged at £20 an hour, and we always tell you the cost before we start.
