# Accessibility

## Getting into our buildings

All three branches have step-free entrances and an accessible toilet. Central Library has a lift to every floor. Northgate Library has no car park, but there is a drop-off space right outside the door.

## Hearing and sight

Every desk has a hearing loop. We have large-print books and audiobooks in all branches. Central Library has a reading magnifier and a computer with screen-reading and magnification software, which you can book like any other computer.

## Home library service

If you cannot get to a library because of illness, disability or caring for someone, our volunteers can bring books and audiobooks to your home every four weeks. The service is free. Phone us to arrange a first visit.

## Assistance dogs

Assistance dogs are welcome in every branch. Other pets are not allowed inside.
