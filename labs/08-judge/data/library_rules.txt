1. Adults may borrow up to 12 items at once; children up to 8.
2. Books are lent for 21 days and DVDs for 7 days.
3. An item can be renewed up to 3 times, online or at the desk, unless someone else has reserved it.
4. Late fees for adults are 25p per day per book, capped at £6 per item. Children's cards pay no late fees.
5. Reservations cost 50p each and are free for children.
6. A lost card costs £2.50 to replace. Bring photo ID.
7. Opening hours: Monday to Friday 9:00 to 19:00, Saturday 10:00 to 16:00. Closed on Sunday.
8. Wi-Fi is free for 3 hours a day. Ask at the desk for a code.
9. Printing costs 10p per black and white page and 40p per colour page.
10. Study rooms can be booked up to 7 days ahead, for at most 2 hours.
